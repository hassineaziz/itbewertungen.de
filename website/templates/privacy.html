This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website under applicable data protection laws is the website operator. If you have questions regarding data protection, you can contact us via email.

2. Server Log Files

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files, which your browser automatically transmits to us. These include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (the page you visited prior to coming to our site)
  • Host name of the accessing computer (IP address)
  • Date and time of the server request

This data is not combined with other data sources. The basis for data processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, which permits the processing of data for the secure operation and performance of a website.

3. Cookie Consent Banner & Storage

We use a lightweight, privacy-focused cookie consent mechanism on our website to respect your preferences. To remember if you accepted or declined tracking, a small parameter named cookie_consent is stored in your browser's local storage.

No tracking or personal profiling starts until you actively give your consent by clicking "Accept" in the cookie settings banner.

4. Google Analytics & Consent Mode v2

If you give consent, this website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies to analyze website usage.

To comply with GDPR/DSGVO, we have integrated **Google Consent Mode v2**. By default, all analytical and advertising tracking storage parameters are set to denied. If you decline consent or do not click the accept button, Google Analytics will not read or write tracking cookies, and any analytical ping sent to Google is fully anonymized.

If you consent, the information generated by cookies about your website usage is transmitted to Google. IP anonymization is active on this website, meaning Google truncates your IP address within Member States of the European Union. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser storage.

5. Affiliate Outbound Links (Cookies)

We participate in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and other affiliate networks. When you click outbound product buttons (e.g. "Preis auf Amazon prüfen"), you will be redirected to the merchant site via an affiliate link. A temporary cookie is set by the merchant to attribute potential sales. This process does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) on our servers.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject in the European Union, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Art. 15 GDPR (Right of access): The right to obtain information about your processed personal data.
  • Art. 16 GDPR (Right to rectification): The right to correct inaccurate data.
  • Art. 17 GDPR (Right to erasure): The right to request the deletion of your data.
  • Art. 21 GDPR (Right to object): The right to object to data processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
